Questions

Quick answers.

Is it hard to learn?

No. Logging cash is one short form: in or out, name, amount, what for. Most people get it on their first try, without any training.

Does my team need to download an app?

No. SpendNote runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer. You invite them by email, they sign in and start logging.

How many people can use it?

The Pro plan includes 3 users for $29/month, and each extra user is $5/month. You can add or remove people anytime. During the 14-day trial, everything is unlocked.

Can each person see only their own cash box?

Yes. Admins see every cash box. Regular users only see the cash boxes you give them access to.

Is this accounting software?

No. SpendNote records who handled the cash, when and why — with receipts. It's not a tax or accounting tool, but you can export everything to CSV or PDF for your bookkeeper.

What happens after the 14 days?

Nothing automatic — we never asked for your card. If SpendNote works for your team, pick a plan. If not, just walk away.
