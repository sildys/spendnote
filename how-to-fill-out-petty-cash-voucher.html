How Do You Fill Out a Petty Cash Voucher?

To fill out a petty cash voucher, enter the date, the amount disbursed, the purpose of the expense, and the recipient’s name, then have both the person giving and the person receiving the cash sign it. Number each voucher in sequence and attach the supporting receipt. SpendNote fills in the date and voucher number automatically and captures both signatures on a printable receipt. Traditional Petty Cash Voucher Fields

A standard petty cash voucher requires these fields:

  • Date: When the cash was disbursed
  • Amount: How much cash was given
  • Purpose: What the cash was used for
  • Recipient: Who received the cash
  • Signatures: Both the person giving and receiving the cash must sign

SpendNote receipts include all these fields automatically—you just enter the transaction details and the receipt is generated instantly.
