Questions

Quick answers.

Does this count as proof of cash payment?

A SpendNote receipt records who paid, who received, the amount, the date and what it was for — with signature lines for both sides and a unique receipt ID. It's a clear written record that the cash changed hands. (It documents the payment; it isn't a tax invoice.)

Do I need to download an app?

No. SpendNote runs in your browser on any phone, tablet or computer. Sign up and make your first receipt in a couple of minutes.

Can the other person get a copy?

Yes — print two copies on one page, hand over a PDF, or email it to them directly. You keep the original in SpendNote.

Nothing automatic — we never asked for your card. If SpendNote is useful, pick a plan from $19/month. If not, just walk away.
