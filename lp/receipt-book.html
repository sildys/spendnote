Questions

Quick answers.

Can I still give people a paper receipt?

Yes. Print two copies on one page, both sides sign, and each keeps their signed copy. You can also save it as a PDF or email it instead. Either way, the receipt is saved in SpendNote so you can find or reprint it later.

Are the receipts numbered like my receipt book?

Yes. Every receipt gets its own number automatically, per cash box, so each book keeps its own sequence.

Is this an invoice or a tax receipt?

No. SpendNote receipts are simple proof that cash changed hands — the same job your paper receipt book does. They are not invoices or tax documents.

Do I need to download an app?

No. SpendNote runs in your browser on any phone, tablet or computer. Sign up and write your first receipt in a couple of minutes.

What happens after the 14 days?

Nothing automatic — we never asked for your card. If SpendNote is useful, pick a plan from $19/month. If not, just walk away.
