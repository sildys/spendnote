Questions

Quick answers.

Is this a security deposit agreement?

No. A SpendNote receipt is proof that cash changed hands — who paid, how much, when and what for. It is not a lease, a deposit contract or legal advice. Local rules on holding and returning deposits still apply.

Can I record when the deposit is returned?

Yes. Log the return as cash out with its own receipt, signed by both sides. The deposit and the return sit in the same history, so anyone can see what came in and what went back.

What about deposits paid by card or bank transfer?

SpendNote is built for cash. When a deposit arrives by card or transfer, your bank already keeps the record — cash is the one that needs a receipt.

Can I still give them a paper receipt?

Yes. Print two copies on one page, both sides sign, and each of you keeps a copy. You can also save it as a PDF instead.

Is this an invoice or a tax receipt?

No. SpendNote receipts are simple proof that cash changed hands. They are not invoices or tax documents.

What happens after the 14 days?

Nothing automatic — we never asked for your card. If SpendNote is useful, pick a plan from $19/month. If not, just walk away.
